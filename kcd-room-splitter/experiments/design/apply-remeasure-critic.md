# Critic of the plan and the wall-record spec (same workflow)

## wrong claims

- PLAN 3.2 default anchor='start' (the START/P0 stays, the END and everything downstream move) is contradicted by the engineered files. Replaying each engineer's length change on the design room with resize_wall (audit3.py): anchor='start' reproduces the engineered points on only 1 of 8 pairs (PARINI 8->18, where the changed wall IS the 0-degree main wall); it mis-places the whole room by dL on POLONSKA (65 raw), SFERRA (159), BINDLER (762), LONG DANI (127), LONG AUSTIN (32). The rule that fits is: the START of the 0-degree main wall (the room origin, (0,0) in 20 of 22 jobs, (64,0) in BINDLER) never moves; a wall upstream of it (reached via left_id, e.g. BINDLER wall 2, SFERRA wall 2, LONG DANI wall 3, LONG AUSTIN wall 9, POLONSKA walls 2/4) moves its START = anchor 'end'; the main wall and walls downstream (via right_id) move their END = anchor 'start'. With that policy 6 of 8 pairs match within the designers' 0-4 raw skew (POLONSKA 2, SFERRA 0, BINDLER 0, PARINI 0, LONG DANI 0, LONG AUSTIN 4). The 2 remaining pairs differ for other reasons: MIRANDA 6->16 (KCD re-derived the edited wall 3's P1 = P0 + L*d, dropping the 16-raw skew; resize_wall does P1 += v and keeps it), ROBERTS 9->20 (a new 5 1/2 in wall 1 was added at wall 13's start, changing its mitre). The committed kcdwalls.py and the CLI (--anchor default 'start') carry the same default.
- PLAN 3.4 ('KCD likely recomputes the unit room points A/B from X/W/wall on load; v1 leaves them stale') is now testable and only partly true. jobs/Cathy Coyne - TEST1 saved by KCD.job (7,507,246 bytes, written 02:08, KCD's re-save of out/Cathy Coyne - REM 1093610 applied - TEST.job; not mentioned anywhere in plan.md or kcd-deep-dive-notes.md, which still says 'waiting for the KCD round trip') shows KCD rewrote block bytes 40..63 and the mesh slice 16..39 on 27 of the 30 units in the three touched rooms, and 26 of 30 then satisfy B_along = X + W/2 from P0 and B_depth = D/2 (fillers: I14 + D/2) against the walls AS SAVED. But 4 units came back inconsistent with their wall by exactly a wall translation: units 10 and 11 (Tall Fillers, type 230, Linen wall 7) kept depth 7192 = 7239 - 47 (wall 7 sits at y = 47 in both the TEST and the KCD file); unit 29 (type 1000, wall 19) is 143.5 raw off along the wall (the TEST's -143 translation of wall 19 was never applied to it); unit 98 (type 1000, wall 10) is 953 raw off along and 79 off in depth (KCD moved the unit by its own re-anchoring vector (-953,+79) although the TEST had not moved it). So 'KCD recomputes' holds for cabinets but not reliably for fillers/accessories; whether a stale A/B is visible or harmful is still unknown.
- SPEC line 38 'P1 = END of inner face = P0 + L*d (high)' holds exactly on only 96 of 120 live walls; 24 differ by 1-4 raw (POLONSKA, ROBERTS, LONG, BINDLER wall 6: designer skew, preserved byte-for-byte into the engineered files 13/19/20), and MIRANDA wall 1 differs by 16 raw in BOTH job 6 and job 16 (L = 39814 = 156 3/4 in, |P1-P0| = 39830; its units end at 39846 = L + 1/8 in). check_room() therefore flags 4 of 29 rooms at the default tolerance (jobs 6, 9 room 2, 16, 20). The spec should state the tolerance and that the engineered files show KCD preserves such skews unless the wall itself is edited (MIRANDA eng wall 3: edited +32 raw, skew removed; wall 1: untouched, skew kept).
- SPEC/self-test line 'byte 15 flag ... sticks with the record slot' is only partly backed. It holds slot-for-slot in PRIEM 2/12, HUMPHREY 7/17, BINDLER 5/15, PARINI 8/18, MIRANDA 6/16 (4 slots) and LONG 10/22 (6 slots), but differs in GODDARD 1/11 (slot 4: 1 -> 0), ROBERTS 9/19 (slot 4: 1 -> 0, slot 9: 0 -> 1) and LONG 10/21 (slots 5,6,10,11). PRIEM shows it is independent of the wall's live state: slots 1-5 are dead in job 2 and live in job 12 with the same values [0,0,1,0,1]. Safe to copy, meaning unknown.
- SPEC line 51 'f64 colour triple == bytes/255 or all zero' fails on 4 dead records: (7,6) and (17,6) hold 0.372549 = 95/255 (a grey never present in bytes 32-34), (9,15) and (19,15) hold 0.882353 = 225/255 while bytes 32-35 are 00000000. Stale colour from an earlier life of the slot; harmless, but the claim as written is false on 4/178.
- KNOWN FACT in the task text 'several Int32s in 48..79 look like 16.16 fixed point (1510604800 = 23050<<16, -74842113 = -(1142<<16)+65535, 1585577983 = (24193<<16)-1)' is an artefact of reading Int32s at offsets 52, 56, ... when the four points sit at 50..81 (x,y pairs at 50/54, 58/62, 66/70, 74/78). audit1.py section 5 shows every such value = (Int32 at o+2) << 16 | high 16 bits of (Int32 at o-2). There is no fixed-point field; the fact should be retired so nobody searches for one.
- PLAN 1 / risk 6 understate the evidence for 'printed wall number = Int32@125 id': COYNE's sheet lists #7..#19 with printed lengths that match ids 7..19 to the 1/16 in on all 12 printed values (6414, 8207, 16526, 16812, 16891, 9271, 18463, 14716, 23241, 603, 15891, 8430), skipping the dead slots 1-6 exactly as the ids do; PRIEM's sheet 'wall 10 = 57 1/4' matches design id 10 = 14542 uniquely (not the engineered file, whose ids are 1-5). Three jobs, not two; the real caveat is that the numbers are the ids of the file the plan was printed from (GODDARD/PRIEM engineered files renumbered).
- PLAN 3.5 'notes: v1 does not move notes; space flag not decoded' - the KCD re-save shows the two spaces directly: Primary's room-level notes (wall field 0: 'Electric Panel', 'HOLES', the kind-5 dimension, 'ROOM DETAILS') moved by exactly +3464 raw in y together with walls 15-19, while every note attached to wall 16 (kind 4/5/6/2) kept its header coordinates although wall 16 moved +3464 too.
